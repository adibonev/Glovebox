# ADR-0008 — Product name: Glovebox

**Status:** Accepted (supersedes the working name "CarGuard")

**Context.** "CarGuard" is generic and already used in the automotive space (incl. a US
vehicle-warranty company and a Zimbabwean vehicle-security firm), with a security/warranty
connotation and trademark/SEO/store-name risk.

**Decision.** Name the product **Glovebox** — the car's glove compartment, where documents
live; the app is a digital glovebox for your vehicle's documents and deadlines. Logo: the
"Glovebox" wordmark with a steering-wheel "o"; app icon = the steering wheel
(`brand/glovebox-wheel.svg`).

**Consequences.** More distinctive and ownable. Check `.com`/`.bg` domain and App Store /
Google Play name availability + trademark before public launch.
